# Caption guide

Built from every reference video sent (Download_4–28 and the b/c/d/e sets) and vidIQ
outlier searches of TikTok/Reels (posts beating their creator's median by 5–2000x, Oct 2026).

## The one rule

The caption names **one situation everyone has been in**, and the footage shows **exactly that
situation happening**. If someone watching on mute couldn't tell what the clip has to do with
the caption, the video doesn't work.

## Openers that work

| Format | Reference / outlier |
|---|---|
| `How dudes be [action] when [situation]` | "How dudes be arguing about who goes into class first when they are 2 hours late" (own post) |
| `Dudes will genuinely [action]` / `Guys will be grown ass men [action]` | "Dudes will genuienly be handsome asf taking a walk in nature" (own post) |
| `POV: [situation]` | "pov: when your daydreaming in class and then you realise everyone is writing" (2.2M) |
| `When [situation] but [twist]` | "When i see my bus leaving but can't run cuz i have to stay nonchalant.." |
| `Me and bro [situation]` | "Me and bro still talking shi to each other in 50 years:" (4.1M) |
| `How fast bro [action]` / `never seen him move so quick` | "pov: how fast bro leaves you for a girl 💅" (3M) |
| `The type of shi dudes be doing [when]` | "The type of shi dudes in France be doing on a regular Tuesday afternoon" (own post) |
| `Me if there was an award for [trait]` | "Me if there was an award for being the most chill nonchalant mf" |
| `[THING] AURA +[number]` | "GOALKEEPER AURA +2000" (68M) |
| `Fastest [group] in [place]:` + a label per person | "Fastest Hussers in Huss Valley:" (4.1M) |

## Writing it

- Keep it universal: no ages, exact times or exact numbers unless the number is the joke.
- Write like the account types: capital first letter, real apostrophes (you’re, it’s), "bro",
  "dudes", "mfs", "lowk", "talm bout", "on sum nonchalant shii", the odd natural slip.
- CAPS for one word of emphasis: STILL, ONE, FINALLY.
- End relatable/skit captions with 😭 or 💔 (nonchalant ones usually have none).
- No family in captions (family watches the account).

## Building the video

- **Relatable / act-out:** one clip (or one clip skipped forward on the beat) doing what the
  caption says. Caption in the lower third. Trending sound, 1–2 mixes at most.
- **Skit:** one premise caption; the clips act it out in order. Only change the caption per
  clip for list formats ("Fastest …: 3. / 2. / 1.").
- **Nonchalant:** walking / staring / posing footage in the square window, caption on the
  footage, phonk-style sound with mixes on the beat, a white flash or black-and-white switch on
  the drop.
- **Rankings / top 5:** Download_13/14 layouts (see `tools/edit_video.py` list styles).
